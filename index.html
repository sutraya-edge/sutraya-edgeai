How it works

Comms: each robot broadcasts pose, intent (next cells), priority, battery to peers in range; lossy, no server.
Planning: time-expanded BFS around reservations of higher-priority peers. Priority = load + task + waiting time (aging) + low battery, so nobody starves and deadlocks break by design.
Choke points: lower priority robots yield or step aside; idle robots clear lanes.
Re-routing: blocked cells are sensed locally and gossiped; unreachable tasks return to the pool and are re-auctioned; a failed robot is detected and its task reassigned.
Safety: an onboard proximity layer e-stops any move into an occupied cell, so collisions stay at zero even with packet loss.
